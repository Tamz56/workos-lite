## 4. Boundary and Lineage Review

- Strategic Guidance operates downstream of Explanation AI and Rule Authority, providing structured decision support while respecting sovereign Human Decision Authority.
- Every strategic recommendation requires exact upstream artifact bindings (Calculation Snapshots, Validation Records, Knowledge Claims, Rule Definitions, Findings, Explanations, and User Context) and preserves all qualification and uncertainty boundaries.
- Strategic guidance never converts astrological symbolism into deterministic real-world facts or inevitable fate.
- All real-world user context remains user-owned and distinct from astrological authority.

## 5. Non-Execution, Anti-Fatalism, and Deferred-Decision Review

- Strategic Guidance is strictly advisory; autonomous execution of financial, communicational, scheduling, or operational actions is prohibited.
- Anti-fatalism invariants prevent fear-based urgency, fatalistic certainty, or the erosion of human agency.
- No recommendation algorithms, scoring formulas, probability calculators, tradition ranking engines, runtime UI, or interpretation-validation test harnesses are authorized.
